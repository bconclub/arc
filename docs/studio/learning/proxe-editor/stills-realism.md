# Stills that don't look AI (six rounds, 2026-10-24/25)

ChatGPT only. Every round was judged by the founder; "still looks like AI, so the video will also look like AI".

| Round | What we did | Founder verdict / what was wrong |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 6-frame storyboard grid (ARC A-F), cut into frames | Looks AI; frames are small and soft because six share one image |
| 2 | Grid again, everything inside the clinic, hands spelled out | Better setting, still AI |
| 3 | **One full-size photo per shot**, "unretouched iPhone frame grab" prompt | Clearly more real. But the face changed shot to shot, and two hands stacked one above the other in the consultation |
| 4 | Founder picked the face (the last frame); **uploaded that photo as the face reference** for every other shot | Same woman everywhere. Still "not real enough" |
| 5 | "Hyper-real": pores, blemishes, flyaways, lens smudge, clutter | "Still looks AI. Push it further." The room was still a pretty showroom with flattering light |
| 6 | **Mundane, unflattering, low-effort selfie**: ordinary cramped room, flat ceiling LED, low front-camera angle, head cut off, tilted frame, radiator, sharps bin, glove box, charger cable, ordinary looks | Pending founder review |

## What actually makes it read as real
1. **One photo per message**, never a grid, when the still will be animated.
2. **Prettiness is the giveaway.** Showroom interiors, golden window light, styled hair, model faces and tidy
   composition all read as AI. Ask for an ordinary person in an ordinary room on an ordinary day.
3. Name the phone and its flaws: iPhone front camera at arm's length, slightly below the face, wide-lens stretch on the
   arm, slight shake softness, shiny forehead from overhead light, Apple over-sharpening on hair.
4. ONE light source (house rule) and make it a plain one: the flat ceiling panel, not a window glow.
5. Real clutter specific to the business, and imperfect framing (tilt, cut-off head, off-centre).
6. Avoid list: glow, beauty retouch, glossy hair, golden tones, perfect teeth, magazine/influencer look, bokeh, HDR.

## Consistency and anatomy
- Pick ONE approved face frame and **attach that exact photo** to every new generation ("use the attached photo only
  for who she is"). Words alone drift the face. Keep the whole set in one ChatGPT chat.
- For every frame, say what each hand is doing ("right arm holds the phone and runs out of the bottom-left edge; left
  index finger points down"). Say "exactly two arms and two hands, clearly separate, nothing stacked".
- Avoid poses where a selfie arm and a gesturing hand overlap; a mirror plus a pointing finger stacked hands. Give
  each person one simple action (holding a tablet with both hands).
- Phone and laptop screens: "a plain blank glow, no app, no text, facing the camera straight on" so our coded UI can
  go on top later.
- Check every frame zoomed in on the hands before sending.
