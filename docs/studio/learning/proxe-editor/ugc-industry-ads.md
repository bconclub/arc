# UGC industry ads (PROXe UK)

**Founder, 2026-10-25:** "The second one is too heavy. First one is cleaner. So I think we'll just do UGC for industry."
One owner-selfie UGC ad per industry, all built the same way, so the only variable is the business. Run them as UK
Meta ads and compare cost per enquiry. Made so far: U1 aesthetics clinic (Halo Clinic, Battersea), U2 estate agent
(Hart & Co, Clapham). Next: home improvement, tutoring, dental/physio.

The story ad ("3 clinics. 1 reply.", customer POV, phone screens) was dropped: too heavy.

## The beats (~36s, 9:16, captions on every line, Reels safe zones)
| Beat | Shows | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Hook | Owner selfie in her own business + title "<Industry>:" + small business-name chip | Frame 1 = thumbnail. The industry must read in the first second |
| 2. After hours | Her phone lighting up on the desk of the closed business | No people |
| 3. Pile-up | The V1 film's lost-leads stream: frosted Notice cards stamped "No reply", running clock, red counter | Clinic: counter shows **£ lost overnight**, +£150 per missed enquiry. Estate agent: **enquiries missed** count, no money |
| 4. Morning | Owner arriving with coat + coffee, holding the phone up, tired | "By the time I replied, they'd booked somewhere else" |
| 5. PROXe answers | The business's **own** Instagram inbox: customer on the left, PROXe's reply + slot buttons + "Booked ✓" on the right, "Answered by PROXe" | Never show the customer's phone in an owner-voiced ad (founder called it a mix-up) |
| 6. Full diary | The owner doing the real work with a client (consultation, viewing) | Makes the industry unmistakable |
| 7. Every channel | "PROXe answers on every channel": WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, Calls, Website chat, Email, SMS chips round the orb | Founder asked for this, 2026-10-25 |
| 8. Close | Owner selfie pointing down: "PROXe. Never miss a lead. Ever again." | Tagline from PROXe V1 messaging |
| 9. End card | The V1 end lock-up held ~3.4s | See brand-reuse.md |

## Rules learned
- **People stay inside their own business in every shot.** The India ads did this; the founder flagged UK frames shot
  at home/kitchen/bedroom.
- **Make the business obvious with props** (clinic: treatment couch, syringe trolley, magnifier lamp, sharps bin;
  agency: window of listing cards, keys on hooks, terraced street outside).
- **Money must match the industry's real economics.** "20 missed × £150 = £3,000 a week" was cut: too heavy for the
  clinic and "not how estate agents' numbers work". Show money only where a missed enquiry has an obvious value, and
  show it *inside* the pile-up counter while the voice carries on, not as its own stretched beat.
- **UK ad rules (ASA/CAP):** the owner is a character, not a fabricated testimonial: present tense, no results numbers,
  a small "Dramatisation" line. A real UK client replaces her later.
- Price: no GBP price for PROXe exists yet, so no PROXe price on screen. "From £250" is the fictional clinic's price.
- UK detail: £, UK spelling (enquiry), +44 Ofcom drama numbers (07700 900xxx), UK names and places, portals named as
  enquiry sources without logos (Rightmove, Zoopla, OnTheMarket). Never "GDPR compliant" unless the founder confirms.
