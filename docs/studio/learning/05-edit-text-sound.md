# 05 · The cut: hook, text, sound, real footage

## Hook and story
- Frame 1 = thumbnail (01-house-rules.md). The hook is a change of visuals, not just words; A/B variants look
  different from frame one.
- Open on the problem or the payoff happening, never on an address to the buyer ("if you run a clinic..."). The
  category is clear from the picture by second 3.
- Hype, not calm: fast cuts, a small punch-in per scene, motion every second. Never recite over a still card.
- Show the logo/product when it's named. One point per reel.
- When the reel is "Chef X's menu", show and talk about the chef: open with a montage of him (founder, Calvary).
- Music first, then cut, then animate: set cut points on the track's bars and size each AI move to its slot. Cut
  on the voice's phrases when there's a VO (time each line with silence detection).

## Text on screen
- Two text moments per ad: the hook and the end card. No caption that narrates what the picture already shows.
- Subtitles (when voiced) style A: 2-4 words timed to the voice, the spoken word highlighted, no boxes, soft shadow.
- Captions never on faces; heavy grey boxes look cheap, use a soft shadow or a thin band.
- Safe zones on 9:16: keep text out of the bottom ~35% and top ~14%, and left of the right-hand button column
  (x < ~930 on 1080 wide).
- **Caption honesty:** a caption naming a dish or product must sit over that dish or product. If the footage doesn't
  exist, keep the names on the end card and use a true line over the footage.
- End card: the real logo file, the offer exactly as the client stated it (check what each price covers), date,
  place, one CTA. Hold ~3-4s, not 7s.

## Sound
- Every reel gets music, made in ElevenLabs Music, 2-3 options for the founder to audition first.
- Hold the music down through a setup, drop it on the action; muffle (low-pass) during a freeze moment.
- Trending IG sounds are added in the Instagram app at posting; export with SFX only. Never rip IG audio.
- Voiceover: ElevenLabs. Offer voices; the founder picks. Every visible action has a sound.
- Master around -14 LUFS, true peak -1.

## Editing a client's real footage (Calvary "The Chai", 2026-10-16)
- Pull **all** their reels (we had 135), split each into ~3s shots with a numbered sheet (scene cuts plus long
  handheld takes cut into pieces), then pick by number.
- **Premium only.** Founder: "we are showing low-class chullas... we cannot show all that." Cut wood fires, home
  stoves, cluttered kitchens; keep styled buffets, banquet halls, the chef in whites, guests.
- Fill 9:16 from their 720p sources with a centred crop; for letterboxed photo slideshows, crop the bars first and
  give stills a slow push-in.
- Length: the ad cut is 15-20s; a longer 30s version can be the main reel.
- Footage gaps (no samosa, no chai shot) go in the open questions, not hidden under a caption.

## Delivery
- Draft 720p, master 1080x1920. Check frame 0, a 1-2 fps strip of the whole cut, and the end frame before sending.
- Send to the founder in chat; Drive only when done or when asked.
- Meta upload cap via the ads agent is 10 MB: make a 2-pass H.264 copy at ~9.5 MB.
