## ZenSquare: "The A★ Program" (zensquare.bconclub.com, 2026-10-23)
- Live online Cambridge IGCSE / Edexcel tutoring, 1:1 or small batches, faculty in Marthandam. For Grade 8-10 students
  already scoring A/B, aiming for A★; families planning for the Ivy League, Oxbridge, top universities. Not remedial.
- Promise: "Ivy League preparation starts in Grade 9." Funnel: consult form (parent, WhatsApp, grade, goal, subjects)
  → PROXe + email; WhatsApp button; 3-question quiz.
- Guardrails: only number = "1000s of students taught"; universities are goals, not partners (no crests, no
  "guaranteed admission"); real tutors only (public/tutors/); site photos are stock (not for films).
- Palette #032A55 navy, #1B32A0 royal, #3532E2 electric blue (accent), #A9B8FF, #E9EDFB, white; gradient 135deg navy →
  #0D2F86 → royal → electric. Type Plus Jakarta Sans + Inter. Logos in the site repo `public/brand/`.
- Film look: evening window light, navy room, red-pen past papers, the parent's trust. ARC Studio:
  `/dashboard/studio/zensquare`. Open: target market (India IGCSE metros vs Gulf NRI), real results, tutor footage.
