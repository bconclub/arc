# Stills that look real (ChatGPT)

## The prompt
- Always the A-F blocks from [../../storyboard-prompting.md](../../storyboard-prompting.md), even for one still.
- **[C] Look** that passed for ZenSquare: "Real candid photograph, not a render. Shot on 35mm film, Kodak Portra 400,
  natural grain. One motivated light source: soft evening window light from camera left, soft natural shadows, no studio
  lighting, no rim light. Colour grade: clean and calm, slightly desaturated, [brand colours] in walls, cloth and
  notebooks... true skin tones. Real texture: skin pores, fine hair, paper fibre, pencil smudges, a worn desk edge.
  Lived-in [city] apartment, not styled or staged."
- **Brand colour lives in the world** (a navy wall, navy notebooks, a navy t-shirt), never as a graphic. This made the
  six frames feel like one brand without any logo.
- **[E] every frame** = shot size + angle + lens (24-35mm place, 50mm people, 85mm faces, 100mm macro) + action + where
  the light falls. Vary sizes across six frames (close-up, over-the-shoulder, macro, medium, close-up, wide).
- Never write "cinematic", "8K", "ultra-detailed", "mist", or stack light sources. That is what reads as AI.
- Earlier finding (T17): "too AI" is the light and styling, not the model. "Indistinguishable from a real, unedited photo
  a colleague took on an iPhone... flat overhead light, mild noise, a little off-level" + ordinary people passed.

## Consistency across six frames
- One ChatGPT chat per brand/film. Frame 1 carries all the blocks; later frames say "keep exactly this girl, room, light
  and grade" and give the new shot.
- Repeat the cast description word for word when a person reappears (Person B: "Indian woman, mid-40s, medium-brown
  skin, shoulder-length black hair, white cotton kurta with a navy border, reading glasses pushed up on her head").
- Ageing a character (ZenSquare frame 6, "now about 18, same face") worked in the same chat.
- When ChatGPT offers two images ("Which do you like more?"), pick the one that matches the board (navy wall behind)
  and tell it which before the next prompt.

## Product truth and physics
- Write what really appears, with geometry: "a plain silver laptop with no logo on the lid", "printed A4 exam papers,
  handwritten working in blue ink, marks ticked and circled in red pen", "a plain black scientific calculator".
- Say "no logo on the laptop" or ChatGPT draws an Apple logo. Say "no crests, flags, signage" on campuses.
- **Phones face the person reading them.** For a parent reading a report: "the phone screen faces away from camera, we
  see only the back of a plain phone". For the user to see a screen, shoot over the shoulder (SecondSpring: the founder
  rejected a still where the phone screen faced the wrong way).
- AI handwriting/maths is wrong under inspection (ZenSquare frame 3 ticked wrong answers). Fine for a look; for a film a
  teacher will watch, use a real worked paper.
- Vehicles and real objects must be exact; once a still gets it right, reuse it as the reference.

## Check before sending
Real photo at a glance? One light? Five fingers? Same person, clothes and room as the last frame? No logos or readable
text? Brand colours present in the room? Then send it to the founder with one line on what the frame does in the story.

## Locking the founder's face across stills (added 2026-10-30)
ChatGPT redraws a face every request, so a real person drifts. What the founder said: sheets 1-2 "are at least close to me", later ones
"too different". What we do now:
1. Two approved sheets are the identity anchors (plaid, navy blazer), plus 4-6 real photos. Attach BOTH anchors in EVERY request, not only the first.
2. New outfits come from EDITING an anchor sheet ("keep all 13 photos identical, change only the clothing"), not from a fresh prompt.
3. Outfit references are cropped below the chin (headless) so another person's face cannot leak in; a full-face outfit photo pulled the face
   toward the model.
4. Keep the face big in frame (a small face drifts most); check every output against the anchors before it counts, label failures DRIFTED.
5. Real footage of the founder is the zero-drift option when it exists.

## One locked face across a whole film, and twins (added 2026-11-02, PROXe The Other You and The Interview)
- A grid board can't hold one face. Lock a master still, then generate frame by frame, attaching the master each time.
- Twins: make the second man from the first man's frame, with one difference only (glasses). ChatGPT redraws the whole image, so
  "unchanged" is never quite true: compare at full size.
- Tight head-filling close-ups crinkle the skin. Chest-up medium close-ups held up. Check subagent output yourself, one frame came back wrong.
