# Code-made app films and explainer reels (10-11 Nov 2026)

Four Instagram references the founder sent, and what we built from them. Notes come from frames, captions and counts.
None of them was listened to. Links only, nothing copied.

## What we took from them
- One flat UI card or UI piece per beat on a solid brand colour, 1 to 2 s each, with a 2 to 4 word line.
- A big cursor or tap does the action, so the viewer sees the app being used without a full screen recording.
- The film ends on one ask: comment a keyword and get something. Comments are the lead list.
- Volume comes from code: one brand kit, a small scene library, then each reel is a headline, a scene and a script.
- All of it is our code-film method (a `seek(t)` page and our renderer), so paid generation is $0.

## What we built
- **BCON app showcase v01** (24 s, EDITR `content/bcon/app-showcase/motion`): title and three phones, then "Your
  store.", "For your people.", "Open to all.", the outcome and price line, and the BCON end card. Ink and lime, our own
  made-up apps. Still silent; price, keyword and music are the founder's to give. Text near the bottom edge sits in
  Instagram's interface zone and must move up in v02.
- **casso v02** (12.5 s, EDITR `content/casso/motion`): the first film built from the reference library (K0109, L0008,
  K0017, H0123). Lessons: a grey floor with a hard horizon reads cheap, use a darker floor with black fog; keep titles
  off the product; an outline-only opening beat is weak next to a lit hero.

## Brand ad made in code with real clips inside (2026-11-12)
- Job: a 30 s BCON brand ad. Idea in one line: every business has two problems (nobody knows you, nobody answers), we
  fixed both. Simple line drawings for the story, real client clips for the proof, $0 paid generation.
- **Real video inside a code film:** don't seek `<video>` elements in the page. Cut each clip to frames first
  (`ffmpeg -ss T -t 1.3 -vf fps=30,scale=540:960 clips/x/%02d.jpg`), preload and `decode()` them, and draw the right frame
  to a `<canvas>` in `__seek(t)`. Every screenshot is then exact.
- **Check each clip does not cross a cut:** tile frames 1, 13, 26, 39 of every clip before building. Four of six first
  picks crossed a cut.
- 540 px wide sources look fine as a tilted card about 690 px wide with a white border, not full screen.
- Frame 0: title plus all six client cards already on screen, so the thumbnail is the hero.
- Drawings rebuilt as one SVG string per frame are fast enough (30 s at 60 fps rendered in about three minutes).
- Sound: CC0 kit checked by fetching each Freesound page and grepping for the `publicdomain/zero` licence link;
  Freesound's search page lists `data-mp3` preview links, so no login is needed for the previews.
- Music length: a 30 s request can return 39 s. Start the track later so it ends with the film; don't trim or fade it.
- Founder note: when options are offered, each one must be ready to watch. Build first, ask after.

## Voice-led code film: one metaphor, timed to the voice (2026-11-12)

- A brand ad that only said things in text and showed client clips was rejected. What worked: one picture idea that carries the whole pitch (customers as dots, the business as a vessel with three leaks, the leaks get sealed and it fills), a voiceover on top, and only short keywords on screen.
- Order of work: build the picture on guessed beat times, record the voice, then move the beats. Keep every beat time in a few constants at the top of the scene (section starts, cue lists) so retiming is one small edit.
- Word timings: `faster_whisper` (`small.en`, `word_timestamps=True`) plus ffmpeg `silencedetect` for the real pause edges. The transcript can mishear a word; that is a timing tool, not a check of the take.
- A 33 s take fits a 30 s film at `atempo=1.1` and sounds more energetic. Film time = start offset + take time / 1.1.
- Particles in a seek(t) film: no simulation. Give each dot a spawn time and a closed-form path, and decide its fate (leaks or settles in slot n) once at load.
- ffmpeg `alimiter` raises the output back to 0 dBFS unless `level=disabled` is set. Without it the mix measured a 0.0 dB peak; with it -2.3 dB.
- Type a brand name the way it should be said in the TTS box (the spelling on screen stays as the brand writes it).

## Real footage inside a code film (2026-11-12, FishPoint montage)

- **Brand montage from a client's own Instagram:** `yt-dlp` can't list a profile while signed out. Read the reel links from the profile
  page DOM in the browser (`a[href*="/reel/"]`), then fetch each reel URL with `yt-dlp`. Photo posts have no video.
- **Footage inside HTML scenes:** pre-cut every shot as an all-intra 1080x1920 clip (`-g 1`), put `<video>` elements in the page,
  and make `__seek(t)` async: set `currentTime`, await `seeked`, then wait two `requestAnimationFrame`s before the screenshot.
  This lets collage frames, flash cards and beat cuts all be driven by one timeline. Shared engine: EDITR
  `content/clients/fishpoint/montage/engine.js` + `render_seq.mjs`.
- **Overlay-only graphics on a plain ffmpeg cut:** render the page with `omitBackground` PNGs into `qtrle` `argb` .mov, then `overlay`.
- **Heredoc + ffmpeg in a `while read` loop:** ffmpeg reads stdin and eats characters from the next line. Add `-nostdin`.
- **Malayalam VO:** Multilingual v2 has no Malayalam; Eleven v3 does. A loose Malayalam-English mix reads naturally for Kerala audiences.
- **Beat grid without librosa:** an onset-flux tempo search in numpy found the 108 BPM we prompted, and gave the phase.
