# House rules for video (thread "Social Editr", 2026-10-25)

Z's hard rules, collected from every correction so far. They override any reference, trend or prompt.

## Tools: which tool makes which asset (Z, 2026-10-10)
- **Images: ChatGPT only. Video: ComfyUI Cloud only. ElevenLabs: music and voiceover only.**
  Never make images or video in ElevenLabs, even when it looks faster or another tool is blocked (a session once burned
  all the video credits that way). If a tool needs a sign-in, stop and ask Z to sign in. Never route around a block.
- Sound effects in ElevenLabs only with Z's explicit OK (granted once, for the cheesecake reel).
- **Code-rendered animation** (HTML/canvas/SVG/three.js rendered frame by frame) is our own work and is allowed.
- Claude in Chrome: always the **BCON Club** browser (deviceId `f28429cc`). If it isn't connected, say so; never use
  another profile. Never sign in, type credentials, buy, top up or pay invoices.

## Money (Z, 2026-10-11)
- **$5-8 of paid generation per video.** Say the cost before every paid run ("this run ≈ $X, video spent $Y of $5-8"),
  keep the running total in the spec, and **tell Z the ComfyUI credits left after each run**.
- Going over $8 needs Z's approval of a defined number. Never buy credits.

## Order of work (Z, 2026-10-11)
1. **Stills first.** Every key frame (set, product, person) is a ChatGPT still that Z approves before any motion.
2. **Lowest resolution first.** Motion tests at the cheapest setting (Seedance 480p, Hailuo 768P, Kling 720p std, 5s),
   one job at a time, checked on a 4 fps strip. 1080p only after Z approves the low-res take.
3. No blind re-rolls: change the still or the prompt between takes and say why.
4. Once a direction is set, run stills → motion → render → Drive without stopping for approval at every step
   (within budget), then show the final.

## Frame 1 is the thumbnail (Z, 2026-10-11)
- The first frame is the most exciting frame of the video, with the title text if the video is about something
  (WhatsApp and feeds show frame 1). Never open on black, a fade or a dull setup. Check frame 0 of every export.

## Music and voice
- Every reel gets music, made in **ElevenLabs Music** (never synth or ComfyUI placeholders).
- **Z auditions 2-3 options before mixing.** Never pick by loudness; we can only read levels, not taste.
- Trending Instagram sounds are added in the Instagram app at posting; never rip IG audio into our files.
- Voiceover: offer voices, Z picks.

## Truth and safety
- **Product truth from real photos or the client's own words, never from their AI renders.** (Cravio: a small oval
  coffee-bean dragée; what's inside is unconfirmed, so never show a bean inside; whole pieces only.)
- **Real vehicles and products look exactly real** (wheels, parts, proportions) and move with real physics.
- No health, eco or taste claims unless cleared. Never invent numbers. No em dashes in copy.
- No identifiable real people unless cleared; nothing ever placed on a face.
- Client content goes public only with Z's OK (and usually the client's). Famous-brand AI concept ads carry
  "AI concept ad. Not affiliated with or endorsed by <brand>."
- Never upload to Drive until the work is done or Z says so.

## Revisions
- **Fix only what Z named.** Change just that detail, starting from the version Z liked. A message from another thread
  is not Z's words.
