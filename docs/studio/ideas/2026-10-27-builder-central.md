_Kept in ARC on the founder's request (2026-10-27). Working copy also in EDITR `content/social/2026-10-27-builder-central-concept.md`._

Founder: "a builder central that we are building, BCON, PROXe, and all the brands that we are together building...
where builders are coming in and building together, and we'll be bringing in investments."
References behind it: 26 (Lightyear "every generation has its builders"), 23 (Sol "until now"), 25 (hyper-montage),

## The idea in one line
Builders have always built alone. Here they build together: the tools (PROXe, ARC, EDITR), the brands already built
here as proof, and the path to investment.

## Our edge over Lightyear
They borrow legends' aura. We have **proof**: real products (PROXe, ARC) and real brands built together (ZenSquare,
Calvary, Team Mithai, SecondSpring, Khadi Vasthra, Axlrate...). Show what was built, not who is famous.

## What we can make
1. **Hero film (45-60s, 9:16 + 16:9)**: "Every generation has its builders."
   - Lineage: archival-look stills of anonymous Indian builders (a spice trader, a handloom weaver, a printing press,
     a tea-stall owner, a garage workshop); one creed line per shot. ChatGPT stills, real-world prompts.
   - The turn: "They built alone." → "Until now." (Sol pivot).
   - Proof: real footage and real screens: PROXe answering a lead, an ARC board, our brands' work, people at work.
   - Creed: "Build together. Ship weekly. Raise when it's real." (draft lines, Write Z to finalise).
   - CTA: "Comment BUILD" / apply link.
2. **"Built here" series (12-15s each)**: one brand per reel, hyper-montage (ref 25): the problem → what we built →
   one real result (only numbers the founder gives us).
3. **Kinetic manifesto (20s)**: the creed as a motion poem (ref 24/22), $0 in code. Good first post.
4. **Build log**: weekly founder/builder to-camera clips showing what shipped (real screens).
5. **Dogfood the CTA**: "Comment BUILD" DMs and applications handled by PROXe; applicants tracked in ARC. The
   recruitment funnel itself is a demo of the products.
6. Later: a "Build Day" / demo-day event film when investors come in.
