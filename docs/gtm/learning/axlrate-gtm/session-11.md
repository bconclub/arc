## The idea
Sales is creating pain. Pain comes from two places, and both get converted the same way:
1. **Problems they want to solve.** Problem x TEMQ impact (time, effort, money, quality lost) = pain. (Sessions 08, 09.)
2. **Outcomes they desire.** An outcome they don't have yet is also a pain: what is it costing them, in TEMQ, every
   day they don't have it?

## Our communication starts from two lists
- **Problems we solve** for the market and clients.
- **Outcomes we enable** for the market and clients.
Every message, ad, script and call is built from one item on these lists, converted into pain in their numbers. Not
features, not the product.

## PROXe: problems to pain
| Problem we solve | Turned into pain (ask, in their numbers) |
|---|---|
| Leads go unanswered or late (nights, Sundays, busy hours) | How many came in last week with no reply in 5 minutes? x close rate x value per customer = Rs lost |
| Founder or front desk buried in WhatsApps (~100 a day) | Hours a day on replies x what that hour is worth; what didn't get done |
| Follow-ups nobody does | How many "call me later" leads got a second touch? Each one lost = a customer's value |
| Team works the wrong leads | Hours spent on junk leads; good leads that waited |
| No view of what happens to leads after the ad | Ad spend a month vs. how many you can trace to a sale |

## PROXe: outcomes to pain
| Outcome we enable | Turned into pain |
|---|---|
| Every lead answered in under a minute, day and night | Every day without it, X leads wait; Y of them buy elsewhere |
| Team on the right lead, ranked by intent | Today the best lead waits behind the loudest one |
| More bookings from the same ad spend | Same spend, Z% of leads lost before anyone talks to them |
| Founder gets their hours back | The hours spent replying are hours not spent selling or running the place |
| One place for every channel (web, WhatsApp, Instagram, calls) | Leads split across phones and inboxes, nobody sees the whole picture |
