## 1. TEMQ money: only actions that move money towards us
The coach asked why Gravity Academy had a 4 for Money (Actions). It came from PROXe's scoring agent reading Z's notes:
Gravity always said yes to the price and talked about its ad spend. That is wrong twice:
- Saying "OK" to the price is **Words**, not Actions.
- What they already spend elsewhere (ads, tools) is **spend capability**, not commitment. "They might have 100 crores,
  but will they give you 50 lakhs?" We score commitment to us only.

| Counts as a Money action | Does not |
|---|---|
| Loops in their finance team | Agreeing to the price on a call |
| Asks for our GST certificate / vendor registration | Talking about their budget or current ad spend |
| Sends payment terms | Asking for a proposal (that is Time and Effort) |
| Asks for an invoice, our bank details or billing email | "We are ready, send it" with nothing after |
| Acknowledges an invoice with a pay date | |
| Pays (5) | |

Default Money (Actions) is 1 until one of the left column happens. Fix for PROXe's scorer: `a_money` rises only on
those events; price talk goes to `w_money`.

## 2. Every conversation has one outcome ("configuration")
"Have a configuration that operates in the bandwidth of the buyer's knowledge, and move with them." No pushing, no
chasing.
1. **Set the outcome before the call, visit or message.** Start with "close the deal".
2. **Check reality in the first minutes (causal law, sustained contact with reality).** If it will not close today,
   reset to one smaller outcome, said in one line: "Before I leave, he says he understands PROXe." Keep educating
   until he says it.
3. **Read TEMQ live** while you talk: is he asking questions, engaging, giving more time? No questions, only
   listening = low commitment.
4. **Low TEMQ means he sees you as a value provider.** Use VPP: trigger the pain ("Don't you want a steady flow of
   leads?"). When he starts asking questions, commitment went up.
5. Commitment rises in **Time and Effort first, then Money**. When it rises, reset the outcome upward (invoice,
   payment, account details, a visit).
6. **A VPP move must raise TEMQ.** If it goes lower, the lead is not good; stop giving it time.
TEMQ and VPP position are live; they change after every conversation.

TEMQ also tells us how much time, effort and money WE should invest in a lead (e.g. is a visit worth it).

## 3. Problem to pain: Problem x TEMQ impact
Take the problem they name and multiply it by its Time, Effort, Money and Quality impact, in rupees, with them.
Sandeep's example: certification gap, 5 people lose ~1 hour a day, ~Rs 20 lakh a year each, about Rs 800 an hour, so
about Rs 4,000 burned every day. Then ask "How are you feeling about this?"

**Genie Bikez, worked on the call:** 80 to 100 leads a day, about 90% junk, 10 to 20 good. About Rs 2,000 margin per
bike. 20 x 2,000 = **about Rs 40,000 a day at stake**. "You are losing about 40K a day. I am asking for 10K a month to
stop that." Perception today: Genie sees PROXe as a value provider ("optimise the process"); the number moves us to
painkiller ("saves us money").

For PROXe calls: leads a day x share that go unanswered or late x close rate x average deal value = Rs lost a month.
The Lead Leak Calculator does this; use it with their numbers, on the call.

**Leave them unsettled.** Do not give the solution in the same breath. "Let me think on this and come back to you."
If they chase you the next day, the pain landed.

## 4. Reviving a cold lead: the outcome is a reply, not a meeting
"Hi, how are you?" gets nothing. Send a pain trigger tied to what they told us:
- "I was working on this and thought of you..." / "I was in your area and thought of you."
- Then one line on a problem they likely have, in their numbers if we know them, and "want to know more?"
Before writing, reread the last conversations and find their pain. No grip on their pain, no message.

## 5. Free work masks value (correction to Z)
Z has been offering free websites, setup or ads "at the cost of PROXe" and sending payment links to leads who were not
aligned. The coach's read: it is desperation, it dilutes the value, and prospects only listen; they are not elated,
so they never saw the pain. Order:
1. Make the gap felt: "How come you don't have a website? With one, and this tool, you would have had this much
   more revenue."
2. They see the value.
3. Then decide whether to give anything free, tied to payment: "If you go ahead with the payment, I will take care of
   the site."
Never send a payment link before alignment. Value our own team's time in front of them.

## 6. Guarantees are two-way
If asked "can you guarantee this money?": we guarantee the platform (uptime, every lead answered); they guarantee
their team uses it and closes. Like a washing machine warranty, it only holds if you use it.
