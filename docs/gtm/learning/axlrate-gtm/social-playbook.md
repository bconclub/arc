# Daily social: LinkedIn and X

> **Date:** 2026-10-26  
> **From:** Z's request to the Axlrate PROXe GTM session  
> **Use it:** The full-time GTM agent and its content/social sub-agents; Z for the daily 20 minutes


Set 2026-10-26 at Z's request: every day, connect with 10 to 20 people on LinkedIn, comment on 5 to 10 posts, and
post on X. The ARC plan row is `gtm_plans.social` ("Daily social: LinkedIn + X"). This file is the how.

## Why (Axlrate)
- Buyers bet on the person (three positionings: person, offering, company). Z's profile is the trust asset.
- Trust comes from going deep into their problem in public, like a doctor asking the right questions.
- Sustained contact with reality: comments put Z inside the buyer's world every day, not just when selling.
- This is not about us. Comments add to their post; they never pitch PROXe.

## Who does what
| Step | Agent (ARC) | Z |
|---|---|---|
| 08:30 daily kit | Builds it: 15 people to connect, 8 posts to comment on with a draft comment each, 1 X post (+1 reply idea) | Reads it, edits |
| LinkedIn connects | Picks people from the ICP list (Dream 100, Apollo free search, ARC Outreach) with profile links | Sends by hand, from Z's profile |
| LinkedIn comments | Finds posts from ICP owners, investors on our list and people in our niche, drafts the comment | Posts by hand, rewrites in own words when it does not sound like Z |
| X post | Drafts in ARC (Write/Schedule) | Approves; it posts only after Z's ok |
| Log | Records every connect, accept, reply and comment against the account in ARC Outreach | Nothing extra |

Time for Z: about 20 minutes a day.

## Hard rules
- No automation on LinkedIn: no bots, auto-connect, auto-comment or browser extensions that act for Z. LinkedIn bans
  accounts for it, and the profile is the asset. Everything is sent by Z's hand.
- No bot replies, auto-likes or auto-follows on X (existing content guardrail).
- Nothing posts without Z's ok.
- Never pitch in a comment. Never drop a link to PROXe in someone else's comments.
- No invented numbers. A claim needs a source or our own data.

## Voice (Z, founder)
- Journal entries, not marketing copy. First person, plain English, short lines. No em dashes.
- Say what we saw, then what we think. "We called 59 clinics last month. Most missed enquiries happen after 7 pm."
- Specific beats clever: a real number, a real business type, a real hour of the day.
- Warm and direct, never salesy. No "game-changer", "revolutionise", "excited to announce".
- Brand: write PROXe in brand case; say "Proxy". Say "Launching", not "Introducing".
- International-safe where relevant (UK partner): do not say "Indian business owners" when the point is universal.

## LinkedIn connects (10 to 20 a day)
- Who, in order: Dream 100 owners (clinics, coaching, real estate, wellness, multi-branch businesses with 30-100+
  enquiries a day); people Z will call this week; family office partners on the A list; agency owners (partner channel).
- Note (under 300 characters, no pitch), from messaging-kit.md. Example: "Hi {name}, I follow how {city} clinics
  handle patient enquiries. Would be good to be connected."
- After they accept: one message from messaging-kit.md, a question about their world, not the product.
- Pace: stay under about 100 requests a week. Withdraw requests older than 3 weeks.

## Comments (5 to 10 a day)
- Where: posts by ICP owners, investors we will pitch (Artha, Claypond, Titan, Paipal), and founders in WhatsApp /
  customer-experience / clinic / coaching growth topics.
- Shape: 2 to 4 lines. Agree or add one real observation, then one question back. Example: "Same pattern we see:
  the enquiry that comes at 9 pm is the one that books elsewhere by 9 am. Do you route after-hours leads to anyone?"
- Never "Great post!" alone. Never a pitch.

## X (1 post a day)
- Topics, rotating: what we saw on calls this week (anonymised), one number about missed enquiries, a build note from
  PROXe or BCON, a lesson from the Axlrate cohort, a customer outcome (with permission).
- Format: one idea, under 280 characters, or a short thread of 3 to 5 posts once a week.
- Track: replies and profile visits that turn into DMs or demos, not likes.

## Measure (weekly, Friday 18:00 review)
- Connects sent, accepted, replied; comments posted; X posts; conversations started; calls booked from social.
- The only KPI stays aligned leads. Social is judged on conversations it starts with ICP owners.
