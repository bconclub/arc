## Outbound call script (draft, waiting on Z's go)
One agent. PROXe introduces itself as an AI.
1. "Hi {name}, this is PROXe. I'm an AI, and I'm calling to introduce myself. Do you have one minute?"
   (No name known: "Hi, this is PROXe...")
2. One question from their world: "Roughly how many enquiries does your business get in a day, and who answers them
   after hours?" Under about 15 a day: thank them and end the call.
3. What I do: "I answer every enquiry your business gets on WhatsApp, Instagram, your website and calls, in under a
   minute, day or night, and follow up until they book. Just like I'm talking to you now."
4. Take it or leave it: "Is this something you're looking for in this business?" Yes: WhatsApp the 2-minute video,
   the calculator and a booking link, or a 15-minute call with Thanzeel. No: thank them and end.

Rules: says it is an AI in the first line; asks once, offers once; if asked the price, "Rs 9,999 a month with a
7-day trial"; "don't call" means do-not-call; under 90 seconds; log the enquiries-per-day answer, the outcome and the
summary to PROXe. Proposed change (Council #14a / #15): the yes line offers "website + PROXe live in 7 days".

Assignment advice sent to PROXe Core: one agent instead of three (Intro Cold, Intro DM, Follow-up); follow-up is the
same agent with the last call's summary. September baseline: 59 connected, 6 interested.

## Email stack
- A Google Workspace inbox sends. Never cold email from bconclub.com or goproxe.com; use a separate lookalike domain.
- Apollo (connected 23 Oct as brands@bconclub.com): finding and verifying contacts, sequences. At connection: 283
  credits, 160 direct dials, no inbox connected, no sequences, Context Center not set up.
- Instantly: only once we pass about 3 inboxes (warm-up and rotation). Not needed for about 1,200 emails a month.
- Setup order: SPF / DKIM / DMARC, profile and signature with a real address and unsubscribe, 2-week warm-up, connect
  to Apollo, start at 10 a day and ramp to 30 per inbox, verify every email, keep bounces under 3%.
- Sequence: 1 a pain question (no pitch, no link), 2 the Rs number and the calculator, 3 a short case, 4 breakup.
  Stop on reply. Every reply becomes a PROXe account.
