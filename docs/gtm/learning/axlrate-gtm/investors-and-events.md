## Summary (act on these now)

1. **Family offices, top 5:** Artha India Ventures (Damani family, led Cheerio AI's Rs 8 cr seed in Mar 2026, the closest comparable to PROXe), Titan Capital (Kunal Bahl and Rohit Bansal, led ContraVault AI's Rs 5.1 cr seed in May 2025), Dr A Velumani Family Office (led Prodoc AI's hospital patient-engagement seed in Sep 2026), Claypond Capital (Ranjan Pai, healthcare-heavy and the most active Indian family office in AI in 2026), Paipal Ventures (Ajith Pai, Mysuru, small seed cheques into SMB software such as Shoopy).
2. **Events, top 5:** Bengaluru Tech Summit (17 to 19 Nov, BIEC, startup pod Rs 30,000 + GST, free Investor Connect EOI), DIDAC India (25 to 27 Nov, BIEC, schools and coaching buyers), Frontier D2C AI Summit Bengaluru (28 Nov, D2C founders), TiEcon Delhi-NCR (2 to 3 Dec, investor Match Room and Deal Room), Medicall Mumbai (8 to 10 Dec, hospital buyers).
3. **For the 15 Nov sprint** only small Bengaluru pitch nights land in time (31 Oct and 14 Nov). Use the big November events to close deals already in the pipeline and to fill the Dec to Jan pipeline. Do not expect them to deliver new signed accounts by 15 Nov.
4. **For fundraising,** lead with traction: paying logos (Zensquare, Scan2Kare, Gravity Academy), the Rs 9,999/month Core plan, and the "under a minute, day or night" proof. Artha and the Velumani office have already backed this exact thesis.
5. **Cut or deprioritise:** Micromax FO (deeptech, Rs 10 to 20 cr cheques), Credence (now a wealth manager after the Equirus merger), Haran FO (one EV deal), Mirabilis and Telama (recent deals are Series A to C).
