# Craft notes from the first batch (T1 to T10, 5-6 Oct 2026)

What the first ten BCON recreations taught. Apply these by default.

## Prompt pattern that worked (Poster-to-3D, Kling; same shape works for Seedance)

Base block on every shot, then one line per product:

> Create a high-impact, premium 3D commercial from the uploaded poster. The uploaded poster is the only source of truth. Keep the product, packaging, logo, text, typography, colors and proportions exactly as shown. No redesigns, no invented features, no new or altered words, numbers or prices. Concept: the poster physically breaks apart and the product bursts fully into 3D space, not a parallax effect, not a zoom, not a simple animation. The product must visibly cross the original 2D plane and become a full-scale, physically real object.
>
> Shot timing (5s): 0 to 1.5s breakout ... 1.5 to 4s hero orbit, camera arcs 30 degrees ... 4 to 5s resolve, product settles centred and still.
>
> Negative intent: no morphing of letters, no extra text, no new logos, no people, no hands.

Why it works: one source of truth (the first frame), an explicit "not a parallax / not a zoom" to stop lazy motion, timed beats, and negatives written as plain sentences.

## Fixes the founder asked for (do these up front)

- **BCON wrapper on every reel:** the BCON end card at the end of every cut. A reel without it is not finished.
- **Real third-party signage got blurred** (FOOH street shot). Generate streets with invented shop names from the start.
- **Screens and packs must read:** on the billboard version the pack was too small and a screen was unreadable. Make the hero product big in frame, and keep UI text legible at phone size.
- **Material effects need depth:** the sand imprint was too shallow until the engraving was made deeper and a real wave and foam wipe was added. Push physical effects further than feels needed.
- **Full 9:16 and legible menus** beat the reference (the pop-up menu ref was letterboxed and tiny).
- **Sound design** was the biggest upgrade over most references: music bed, a hit on every reveal, ambience under street scenes.
- **QC pass** caught warped text, inconsistent products and IG UI collisions on every reel. Never skip it.

## Effects that recur

| Pattern | Core move | Stills | Motion |
|---|---|---|---|
| Poster to 3D | product breaks out of a flat poster | poster per product | first frame = poster, timed breakout |
| Billboard hand-off (FOOH) | snap, person in ad hands product out | street plate with billboard | first = static billboard, last = product in POV hand |
| Pop-up / holo menu | menu unfolds in 3D on a table | table plate + menu art | slow push in, unfold, settle |
| Storefront logo | before still, prompt, after clip, stacked | blank storefront + material refs | sign builds itself in the material |
| Ingredient fill | outline on monochrome set fills with ingredient | outline still + filled pack still | first = outline, last = full pack, loop back |
| Product rain | specks fall and become the range | empty set + final range frame | fall and grow, end on range |
| Material imprint | logo drawn into sand or spice, wave wipes it | carved still | draw, hold, wave wipe to loop |
