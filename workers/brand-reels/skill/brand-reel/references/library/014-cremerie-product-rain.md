1. 0 to 2.5s: a tiny cluster of dots far away in the sky.
2. 2.5 to 4s: they fall toward the camera, growing: hair mist bottles (Rose Macaron, Butter Croissant, Santal Sucré, Vanilla Royale), tortoiseshell "Paris" claw clips, a striped pouch.
3. 4 to 5.6s: the products fill the whole frame in a slow-motion pile-up, labels legible, then hold.

## Rules it follows

1. **The caption pun IS the shot** ("It's raining hair mists"). Copy and visual are one idea.

3. **Whole range at once** (4 scents + accessories) = giveaway and range shot in one.
4. **Ultra short (5.6s)** = rewatches; ends on a dense, screenshot-able frame.
