# 010: Billboard hands you the product, AI FOOH (Malik, @nerfisking)

- **Source:** https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dc1fyuqADLQ/ . 1076x1914, 46s, music only.
- **Category:** AI FOOH (faux out-of-home) > Billboard Hand-Off > POV
- **Caption hook:** "What if a billboard could hand you the product? Snap, billboard comes alive. No crew. No physical billboard. Just AI + creativity."

## Composition

6 brands x ~7.5s, same 4-beat micro-story each:
1. **Establish:** a real-looking Lagos street (danfo buses, okadas, market umbrellas, Third Mainland Bridge) with a big billboard ad.
2. **Snap:** a POV hand rises into frame and snaps its fingers at the billboard.
3. **Come alive:** the person on the billboard moves, smiles and reaches OUT of the billboard frame, holding the product.
4. **Hand-off:** the product crosses into the real street and lands in the viewer's hand (Pepsi can, Nivea bottle, Coke bottle, Indomie pack, Milo mug, Maltina bottle). The billboard returns to static.

Watermark @NERFISKING centre. No captions.

## Rules it follows

1. **A gesture triggers magic** (snap). Simple cause and effect, instantly readable, repeatable 6 times.
2. **The product physically crosses the frame** (2D ad into 3D world): same core idea as 005's poster breakout, but outdoors and handed to YOU.
3. **Hyper-local setting** (Lagos streets, Pidgin copy "Hunger no dey wait!") makes it feel real and shareable in that market.
4. **Billboard ads look like real campaigns** (headline, product, claims), so the twist lands.
5. **No VO, no captions:** works in any language.

## Production guess (moderate)

Billboard ad images generated (ChatGPT/Nano Banana style), composited into a generated street plate, then image-to-video (Kling/Veo) with start frame = static billboard, prompt = person reaches out and hands product to POV hand. Possibly end frame = product in hand.

## Reusable for BCON

- Steal: snap-to-life trigger, product hand-off out of the ad, local streets, 4-beat micro-story repeated per brand, silent format.
- Do better: Indian street settings (BCON's market), real BCON clients (Evernuts pack handed out of a billboard) with permission, otherwise invented brands. Skip famous FMCG marks (Pepsi, Coke, Nivea: IP). Add sound (snap, crowd, can crack). End card: "Your brand, off the billboard. BCON."
