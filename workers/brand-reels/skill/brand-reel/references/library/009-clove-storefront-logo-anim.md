- **CTA:** end card "Comment 'Logo' to get the tutorial for FREE"; caption same. Tool: Gemini "Omni Flash".

## Composition

Fixed vertical stack on a dark teal gradient:
1. **Header:** Claude-style asterisk icon + Gemini star + "Logo Animation On Omni Flash".
2. **Top card (before):** still photo of a storefront at night/day with its sign.
3. **Prompt bar:** a short prompt types out live ("make bouncing animation on logo", "make the logos enter one by one and light up", "make the hand appear on the wall", "make the logo light up one by one").
4. **Bottom card (after):** the generated clip plays: letters bounce in, cube letters drop and light up, neon sign types on letter by letter, a red sculpted hand slides a light tube on, serif letters light up one by one.

5 storefronts x ~3s: Cold Co (juice bar), Re-Entry (cube letters on perforated facade), Blockbuster, coffee (white minimal), Barilatte (gold serif). End card CTA.

## Rules it follows

1. **Before, prompt, after in one frame.** The viewer sees input, instruction and output at once; no narration needed.
2. **Prompts are tiny** (5 to 9 words). Sells "anyone can do this".
3. **Each example = a different sign material** (3D letters, lightbox cubes, neon, light tube, gold serif): variety without changing the template.
4. **Physical, plausible motion** (bounce, drop, light-up) keeps it believable; logo stays legible.
