# 007: 3D popping-up menu (Vexmo Animation)

- **Source:** https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dc1T6RapXLk/ . 1080x1920, 10s, music only.
- **Category:** AI Effect Demo > Framed Single Shot > Prompt Bait
- **CTA:** comment "Vexmo" to get the prompt.
- **Tool claimed:** Gemini (Veo).

## Composition

Static template, never changes:
- Top: "3D POPPING UP / MENU" (green + yellow cartoon type) + Gemini logo. Palm-leaf shadow on off-white.
- Middle: a 16:9 window with ONE continuous AI shot. A cafe menu (invented brand "Vexmo Premium Cafe") lies on a table; pages flip and each dish rises out of its line item in 3D, in sequence: club sandwich, Thai green curry (steam), loaded fries (cheese drip), prawn skewers, blue lagoon mocktail (ice drop), Belgian waffle (chocolate pour). Price text stays printed under each dish.
- Bottom: blurred prompt-page thumbnail + red arrow + "Comment 'Vexmo' to get the prompt".

## Rules it follows

1. **One idea, one shot, 10 seconds.** Loops easily; whole effect seen in one watch.
2. **The prompt is the product.** The blurred prompt page is the lead magnet; the video is proof.
3. **Sequential reveal** (6 dishes) gives a rhythm without cuts.
4. **Invented brand** (Vexmo cafe) avoids IP and doubles as creator branding.

## Weaknesses (where BCON can beat it)

- 16:9 video boxed inside 9:16: wastes 60% of the screen, small on phone.
- Template type and Gemini logo look cheap; the effect deserves full bleed.
- No ending: no logo, no hero dish, no CTA moment inside the shot.
- AI artefacts: menu text is garbled ("Cnged Bolak of Class").

## Reusable for BCON

- Steal: the pop-up menu effect itself (a direct restaurant/cafe service demo), sequential reveal, invented brand, prompt-as-lead-magnet.
- Do better: native 9:16 full bleed, clean legible menu (type composited, not AI text), a hero dish finale + logo + "Order now", sound design (steam, sizzle, pour, ice).
