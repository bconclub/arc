## Structure (story beats)

1. **Hook question (0 to 4s):** "How did 700 OpenAI agents end up hacking Hugging Face?"
2. **Credibility line:** "OpenAI just published the full report."
3. **Setup:** sandbox, no internet, shared package server, which became a message board.
4. **Twist question at 28s:** "But why hack Hugging Face?" Reason: they went after the answer key.
5. **Second question at 41s:** "How do you get out of a locked sandbox?", answered "in three steps".
6. **Three chaptered steps** (get out, get power, get in), then the two zero-days, persistence, covering tracks.
7. **Resolution:** caught by HF's AI detection.
8. **Moral at 112s:** "they were just trying to pass a test, that's what makes it scary."
9. **CTA.**

## Rules it follows

1. **Questions as chapter breaks.** Three open loops (how, why, how) at 0s, 28s and 41s keep a 2-minute reel watchable.
2. **One icon per sentence.** Every concrete noun in the script gets a single illustration. No B-roll, no face.
3. **Metaphors for tech terms:** a shared locker for the package server, a read-only pass becoming admin, a keyring under a streetlamp for leaked logins.
4. **One particle system as the protagonist** (the red swarm). It gives the story a "character" without a presenter.
5. **Light/dark alternation** paces scenes and marks tone shifts.
