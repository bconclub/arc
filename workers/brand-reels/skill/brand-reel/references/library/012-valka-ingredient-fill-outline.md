# 012: Pomegranate fills the LipButter tube (Valka, @valka_ai)

- **Source:** https://www.instagram.com/reel/DdR4sJPTYjP/ . 1080x1920, 11s, music only.
- **Category:** AI Product Film > Ingredient-to-Product > Fill-the-Outline Loop
- **Tool (caption):** Higgsfield MiniMax H3. Unsolicited spec ad for Kylie Skin LipButter Pomegranate.
- **CTA (caption):** "Brands, ready to turn your product into a scroll-stopping AI campaign? DM to work together."

## Composition

Locked camera, monochrome coral set (background, floor, product all one hue), pomegranates either side.
1. 0 to 2s: an empty line-drawing OUTLINE of the tube stands centre.
2. 2 to 4s: a hand enters top with a pomegranate half.
3. 4 to 8s: the hand squeezes; juice pours into the outline and fills it from the bottom; as it fills, the liquid turns into the solid tube with label (Kylie Skin LipButter, Pomegranate) resolving bottom-up.
4. 8 to 10s: the hand leaves; hero product beauty hold.
5. 10 to 11s: product fades back to the outline: perfect loop.

## Rules it follows

1. **Ingredient becomes product.** One visual sentence: "made of pomegranate".
2. **Outline to solid** is a built-in progress bar (fill level) and a satisfying payoff.
3. **Monochrome set** in the product's own colour: premium, zero clutter, label reads.
4. **Loop by design:** the end returns to frame 1.
5. **Business model:** spec ad for a famous brand, posted as portfolio, CTA to brands. This is exactly the BCON pitch play (with a client we can legally show).
