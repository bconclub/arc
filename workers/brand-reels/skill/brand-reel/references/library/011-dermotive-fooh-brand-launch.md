# 011: Dermotive Prime+ Mask, billboard toss (brand's own ad)

- **Source:** https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dc1R-fBREMA/ . 1080x1920, 10s, music only. Posted by the brand Dermotive (Arabic caption: Prime+ mask presale, pre-order, link in bio).
- **Category:** AI FOOH > Billboard Hand-Off > Single-Brand Launch
- **CTA:** on-billboard "Prime+ Mask / in stock soon / Pre Order Now" + caption pre-order.

## Composition

One continuous handheld "phone" shot (slight shake, push-in, tilt), 10s:
1. 0 to 2s: wide street, giant curved LED billboard on a glass tower, model in white/blue drape with the jar on a white plinth.
2. 2 to 6s: camera pushes in; model picks up the jar, turns it, shows the lid.
3. 6 to 7.5s: she tosses the jar OUT of the screen toward camera; it flies over the street.
4. 7.5 to 10s: a real hand catches it; rack focus to the jar (label legible: Dermotive, Prime+ Mask, Glow Mask, 100ml); billboard behind shows "Prime+ Mask in stock soon, Pre Order Now".

## Rules it follows

1. **One shot, one product, one action.** Simpler than 010's 6-brand compilation.
2. **Handheld phone language** (shake, imperfect framing) sells "someone filmed this on the street".
3. **Hero packaging ends sharp and legible** in hand with a real-looking label: the shot ends on the actual product.
4. **The billboard carries the offer copy**, so no overlay text is needed.
5. **A real brand ran this as its launch ad**, so the FOOH hand-off isn't just a creator trick, it's a deliverable brands buy.
