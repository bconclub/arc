# Brand profiles

Use the profile for `job.json.brand`. The team's brief beats anything here. If the brand is not listed, read the brief, keep it neutral and premium, and note the assumptions in NOTES.md.

## BCON (BCON Club, bconclub.com)

- **What it is:** Human x AI marketing and education. BCON makes the content; the reels show what Human x AI creative can do for a brand.
- **Reel job:** show off a creative mechanism so a founder thinks "I want that for my brand". The product is the craft itself.
- **Default subject when the brief is empty:** invented spec brands from Indian everyday life (snack, chai, sneaker, scooter, cafe, salon, skincare, real estate), each with an original name and pack. BCON's own logo appears in the finale and end card.
- **Market and setting:** India. Indian streets, shops, signage in English or Hindi, Indian faces and hands.
- **Look:** near-black `#0A0A0A` base, lime `#CCFF00` accent (CTA pill outline, highlights), violet glow `#6B2FE8` used sparingly. Type: Inter (700 to 800) with one Fraunces italic word for emphasis.
- **Logo:** `endcard/assets/BCON_logo-white.png` (white on dark).
- **End card:** logo, one short line, lime outline CTA pill, "HUMAN X AI" small at the bottom (the end card template does this). Example lines: "Your brand, off the billboard.", "Posters that <em>move</em>." CTA examples: "DM 'REEL'", "bconclub.com".
- **Voice:** confident, playful, specific. No AI buzzwords ("revolutionary", "game-changing", "unleash"). No em dashes.

## PROXe (goproxe.com)

- **What it is:** conversational AI for small businesses: an AI agent that answers leads on WhatsApp, web chat and calls, books appointments, follows up.
- **ICP:** Indian solo founders, clinics, academies, real estate, tutoring businesses.
- **Reel job:** make the pain (missed leads, late replies, manual follow-ups) visible, then show PROXe handling it. Real-looking chat UI and phone screens work well.
- **Look and logo:** no logo file ships with the worker yet. Use a clean wordmark "PROXe" in Inter 800 on a dark end card unless the brief supplies assets.
- **Voice:** same rules as BCON. Concrete outcomes ("replied in 4 seconds at 11pm"), never vague claims. Never invent customer names, numbers or testimonials: label anything synthetic as a demo.
