What gets ads pulled

Meta and under-18s

Advertisers can't target teenagers by interests, only by age and location. Ads that speak to parents avoid the problem.

Claims about results

India's ad code (ASCI) asks education ads to back up claims about results and toppers, and to have consent for names and photos.

University names and crests

They belong to the universities. Use them as goals, never in a way that suggests a tie-up.

Guarantees

No guaranteed grades or admissions. Your FAQ already says no one honest can.

Many ads in this market lean on percentages and university logos. Keeping to real tutors and a clear method stays safe.
