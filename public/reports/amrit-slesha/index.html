What gets ads pulled

Indian law

The Drugs and Magic Remedies Act: no cure or magic claims, including for herbal products.

Meta

No cure claims, no copy implying it knows the viewer's health, no targeting by health condition.

Google

Unapproved products can't be marketed as safe or effective against a disease.

Your website

Ad platforms can review the page an ad links to, so its wording counts too.

Sources: Meta Ad Library, Google Ads Transparency Center, Google Keyword Planner, Google Maps, Google healthcare policy, summary of Meta health rules (Wittelsbach), Drugs and Magic Remedies Act overview. A market reading, not legal advice.
